# Design ideas (not built yet)

Drawings from Dallas for parts still to come, kept here so they can be built from later.

## temperature-switch-NO.png: temperature switch, normally open (2026-11-02)

- The body is the limit switch's (bezelled, rounded square), with its tags in the bottom corners: **TS1** (its
  number) bottom left, **NO** bottom right. No domed head or roller lever on top.
- Inside, the contact as on the limit switch: the two round posts (stubs out to each side), and the blade hinged on
  the left post, hanging open below the right post (normally open).
- Under the blade, in place of the limit switch's wedge, the NEMA temperature switch's actuator: a stepped,
  zig-zag stem hanging down from the middle of the blade.
- On its right, a second, smaller rounded box joined to the body (a sensing bulb's housing), holding a
  thermometer: a bulb at the bottom and a column, red up to the temperature, grey above it. The right-hand
  terminal's lead runs out through this side box.

## O3SwitchDesign.drawio: the switch designs, editable (2026-11-02)

The draw.io source for these drawings (open it at app.diagrams.net). It holds the limit switch as built (body with
its domed head and roller lever, the dashed link, LS1 and NO tags) and the normally open temperature switch above,
with the NEMA symbols embedded as SVG: the limit switch's hinged blade and wedge, and the temperature switch's
blade with its stepped stem hanging below it (small rectangles in a 40 x 40 symbol, like Dallas's other "Symbol
Design" files). The thermometer is drawn from draw.io shapes: a grey column above a red one, ending in a red bulb.

# Backups

Saved copies of the Circuit Builder (and, from Nov 10, 2026, the Print Builder), kept only as backups. A backup is saved before every push to main. Nothing on the site links here: the page students use, and
the installed app, is always `Circuit_Builder.html` at the top of the repository.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `2026-10-23/` | The Circuit Builder as of Oct 23, 2026 (before timers): pushbuttons, relay coils and contacts, pilot lights (can be built into a button), copy/paste, speed slider up to 300×, O³ icon. `Circuit_Builder.html` is the complete program (open it in a browser); `Circuit_Builder_source.zip` is its source. |
| `2026-11-04/` | The Circuit Builder as of Nov 4, 2026 (before process values): timers, limit switches, selector switches, motors and solenoids, 3-phase power with single and phase fuses, temperature switches (high and low), the operator station with its speed controls. `Circuit_Builder.html` is the complete program; `Circuit_Builder_source.zip` is its source. |
| `2026-11-06/` | The Circuit Builder as of Nov 6, 2026 (before the wire diagram print): everything above, plus pressure switches, process values and the Process window, the heating element, outputs that drive process values, the resizable operator station and Process window, and parts kept on the grid at the panel's edges. `Circuit_Builder.html` is the complete program; `Circuit_Builder_source.zip` is its source. |
| `2026-11-10/` | The Circuit Builder and the Print Builder as of Nov 10, 2026, just before that day's push to main (before the selection-box polish and Space+drag panning in CB). `Circuit_Builder.html` and `Circuit_Builder_source.zip` (source at commit 234aa65); `print-builder/Print_Builder.html` and `print-builder/Print_Builder_source.zip` (source at commit 118776c: Print Preview, multi-edit, adjustable dashed links, Orientation menu, selection box from the grey and the rest of the 9–10 Nov features).

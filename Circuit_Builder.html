Order of Operations: Circuit Builder

Speed
Pushbuttons
100%

Power comes in from the L1 bar on the left and goes out to the L2 bar on the right. Drag buttons, coils and relay contacts onto the panel, or onto a wire to put them in it. A contact is worked by a coil: pick which one in its settings (it follows its coil a moment after the coil picks up or drops out). Draw wires by dragging from a free end of a part or from either bar (or anywhere, with the Wire tool). Drag the middle of a wire to slide it; drag any wire end, even one fastened to a part, to pull it off and put it somewhere else; when you move a part, its wires follow. A green ring shows where a wire or part will connect. Drag across empty space to select several things (Shift-click adds or removes one), then drag any of them to move them together. Drag the handle at the top of the L1 or L2 bar to make the circuit longer or shorter (double-click it to fit the screen again). Ctrl + scroll wheel zooms; scroll and Shift + scroll move around. Then press Run.
